# AnotherLife

**A life simulator that runs entirely in your browser.**

AnotherLife is a browser game that plays like BitLife. You're born, you press **+ Age**,
and you live a whole life one year at a time. Along the way you go to school, get jobs and get
promoted, date, marry, have kids and adopt pets, buy houses and cars, start companies and invest,
gamble, commit crimes, go to prison and try to escape, get famous, and finally die and earn a
ribbon. Then you can carry on as one of your children.

Every part of the story is built in and runs on your device:

- each year's life-log lines and random events with choices come from a large event library
  that knows your age, country, era, family, job and situation, and doesn't repeat itself;
- **Do Anything**: type any action ("start a garage band with my best friend") and the game
  reads its keywords and works out what happens, within the rules for your age, money and
  whether you're behind bars (a few times a year);
- job interviews draw from a pool of scored questions, and applications are screened against
  your CV and the local job market;
- your obituary is written from the life you actually lived.

Money changes your life too. Once you're rich, your kids want pocket money, cars, vacations and
a house, and friends and family come asking for loans. Companies grow without hard caps: unlimited
R&D and product lines, as many stores, offices and factory lines as the market and your cash
allow, picked with sliders. Hire a CEO to run them (the best ones above skill 75 optimize like
real executives and write you a yearly letter), answer to a board of directors once outsiders
own part of the company, and merge your companies into a conglomerate.

Choose from 51 company sectors. National demand, input costs and customer tastes change over
time; study your markets and run up to three local strategy programs per country. Elite CEOs
bring stronger operating performance and turnaround planning, and ask for an initial 2–10%
equity grant. Any positive company or conglomerate stake prevents voluntary resignation or
retirement, even after dilution or skill growth; salary and board dismissal still apply.
Group CEOs recruit division leaders at their own rating or higher.
Large stock orders affect execution prices and market quotes, and major company declines
shake national-sector confidence.

**Investment firms, banks and online banks** have dedicated financial books, not product units.
Hire specialists, choose portfolio or credit policies, attract clients, manage reserves and
survive withdrawals and defaults. Client investments and deposits are segregated from company
cash and shareholder equity. Inventor-talented owners can develop an original market algorithm
even before computers; its advantage decays and never guarantees returns. Online banks require
local internet and online-banking technology.

With the **Inventor** talent, visit **Activities → Inventor** to study a specific
topic at the library, or online from 1995. Fund research from your personal budget: small
inventions finish instantly; middle, big and generational projects take one, three and five
active years. Completion is guaranteed. The result is personally owned IP, not an unrelated
company demand boost. Assign it to a compatible company or license it for a royalty, then set
the actual product's price and production. Sales require staff, cash and customers. Open the
owned invention's national-patent screen to file in all remaining nations at once; fees,
filing windows and national patent terms still apply. Existing company research queues remain
compatible and complete into manufactured products.

In **Owned Inventions**, build or expand dedicated fabs with company cash, invest in
technology/fab upgrades, and set annual output with a production slider and live forecast.
Any nonnegative selling price is allowed, including free, loss-leading or monopoly pricing;
customers can refuse unreasonable prices. Actual output needs paid capacity, workers and
working capital. New products start without factories; old saves retain their original small
starter operation. Personally or company-owned IP can also be licensed to outside manufacturers:
they fund their own fabs and pay royalties on actual simulated sales in separate customer channels.
Contracts retain IP ownership and last no more than five years or the remaining patent term.

Generational breakthroughs begin with extraordinary demand and generate world news and a
one-time **+1,100% stock-market repricing** across then-open listings, rather than a repeated
annual payout. Existing holdings and quotes agree; buying after the announcement does not
replay the jump. Governments may seek strategic access, cooperation or stolen technology.
Security investment and your responses affect leaks. Demand and monopoly pricing erode as
each nation's patent counts down; expiry allows cheaper competition. Upgrades improve the
technology and economics but never restart the original patent term.

Own a **casino, nightclub, bar or adult entertainment club** where simulated local licensing
allows it. Visit the floor, hear staff concerns, handle an owner request, support performers,
book collaborations and invest in safety or hospitality. All adult venues and encounters are
18+ and consent-gated. **Crime → Drug Markets & Underground Empire** adds fictional regular
and ultra-rich markets, staff-run abstract production, a separate criminal treasury, and risky
money-laundering choices through controlled operating companies. These are fictional game
mechanics, not real substances, recipes or concealment instructions.

Luxury personal staff now include a **secretary**, **estate manager** and **concierge**. A
secretary can screen requests from children, family, friends and strangers into an approval
inbox or decline selected categories; they never spend your money without approval. Family
retreats, patron salons and learning sabbaticals have real relationship, networking and recovery
effects, with concierge discounts and shared annual limits. Landlords
can set renewal rents, manage vacancy, hire property managers, resolve repairs, track arrears
and balance tenant satisfaction against costs. Adults can make up to **24 shared baby attempts
per year**, and unprotected adult hookups can result in pregnancy. **Activities → Backroom
Favors** offers fictional, uncertain and criminally risky attempts to obtain jobs for adult
relatives through political or corporate influence.

Game studios can develop their own technology from **Game Engines** on the company screen:
build an indie, professional, or flagship engine, then select it when greenlighting games for
royalty-free use. Offer three-year licenses for annual fees or revenue royalties, or sell the
engine IP outright. Development uses the studio's existing developers and company cash;
licensing demand follows quality, reputation, engine age, and the market. Selling transfers
future licensing income and new-game rights while preserving use in existing games.

Become an **Author & Storyteller** from **Occupation → Special Careers**. Practice writing
and illustration, then draft novellas, novels, or epic sagas. Japan also offers **Manga**;
Korea and China offer **Manhwa / Manhua**. Serial creators make a promise to their readers:
characters, mystery, or spectacle. Cliffhangers attract attention but build story debt;
payoff arcs earn trust. Balance release schedules, burnout, and hiatuses, then choose a
satisfying finale. Self-publish or accept a recoupable publisher advance, earn back-catalogue
royalties, and license successful stories for screen or animated adaptations while retaining
the original IP. Existing publications keep earning after you leave the career.
The Story Desk lets you name your lead character and setting, choose individual scene beats,
keep a persistent notebook, host editorial reader workshops, and react to reader feedback.
The old “How This Career Works” menu has been removed.

There is no server and no account: the game never sends anything anywhere.

## Play it

AnotherLife is a static website hosted on Cloudflare. Open the game at its Cloudflare URL (see
[Deploying](#deploying-to-cloudflare)), e.g. `https://snag.<your-account>.workers.dev`, and press
**New Life**: a random life, or a custom one (name, gender, country, birth year from 1900 to
today, starting stats and up to three special talents). Your current life, your ribbons and your
graveyard are saved in this browser.

## Deploying to Cloudflare

This repo is connected to a Cloudflare Worker named **`snag`** through Workers Builds. Every push
to the Worker's production branch rebuilds and redeploys the site. `wrangler.jsonc` declares a
static-assets Worker: `wrangler deploy` runs `npm run build` (typecheck + Vite) and uploads
`dist/`, so a build that doesn't compile is never deployed. The site is served at
`https://snag.<your-account>.workers.dev` plus any custom domain attached to the Worker under
**Settings → Domains & Routes**.

To deploy by hand from your computer:

```bash
npm install
npx wrangler login
npm run deploy
```

## Run it locally instead

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

Open <http://localhost:5173>.

## Settings (⚙️)

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Currency** | *Local currency* (yen in Japan, Deutsche Marks in 1980s Germany...) or *US dollars* everywhere. Only the display changes. |
| **Prices** | *As they were*: amounts in the money of the year being lived. *Today's money*: every year in today's prices. |
| **Sandbox: unlimited Time Machine** | Removes the limit on Time Machine jumps (normally 3 per life, to the start of one of the last 10 years). |
| **Danger Zone** | Abandon the current life, or reset your ribbons and graveyard. |

Upgrading from a version that had an AI narrator: your saves keep working, and the old AI
settings (provider, server URLs, API keys, models) are deleted from this browser the first time
the new version starts.

## Development

```bash
npm run dev          # dev server
npm test             # unit tests (Vitest)
npm run typecheck    # TypeScript
npm run build        # production build into dist/ (serve it with `npm run preview`)
npm run deploy       # build and deploy to Cloudflare (needs `npx wrangler login` once)
# Headless playthrough in Chromium (fails on page errors, console errors or any outside request):
npm i --no-save playwright && npm run build && npx vite preview --port 4341 --strictPort &
node scripts/e2e-play.mjs http://localhost:4341/ --years 120 --seed 1
```

The engine (`src/game`) is plain TypeScript with a seeded RNG and no React; the React UI is in
`src/ui`. See [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) for how the pieces fit together.
