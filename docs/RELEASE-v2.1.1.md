# AnotherLife v2.1.1 — Inventor Career & Real-World Innovation

This release rebuilds Inventor as a personal career with a grounded research,
prototyping, commercialization, and reputation lifecycle.

## Highlights

- Inventor is a talent with developing expertise: study and practical experience
  shape the subjects a character can pursue, including six practical disciplines
  and a broader catalog of research topics.
- Research advances over realistic multi-year timelines. Projects move through
  investigation, prototypes, customer evidence, and independent replication;
  discoveries can fail, need revision, and impose workload and burnout costs.
- Grants are restricted project funding rather than spendable personal cash.
  Paid collaborators receive credit, and rest or imprisonment can pause work.
- Inventors can build reputation through mentoring, conferences, and consulting.
  Credit disputes and promotional claims carry consequences; consulting income
  is taxed only when the offer is accepted.
- Inventions preserve inventor and co-inventor attribution through licensing and
  commercialization. Real adoption and sales influence demand and reputation,
  instead of speculative breakthrough headlines automatically inflating every
  stock in the market.
- Existing invention projects and commercialized products remain compatible
  with the revised system.

See [INVENTOR-CAREER.md](INVENTOR-CAREER.md) for detailed mechanics.
