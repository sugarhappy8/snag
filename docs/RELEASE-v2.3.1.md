# AnotherLife v2.3.1 — Fair CEO Shares

Star CEOs now ask for much smaller stakes, the size depends on the seat they hold, and a CEO who
leaves gives the shares back to you.

## 🤝 What a CEO asks for

Only CEOs rated 85 or more ask for shares. What they ask for depends on the seat:

| Seat | Rated 85 | Rated 90 | Rated 95+ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A conglomerate's own CEO | 2% | 3.5% | **5%** |
| A standalone company's CEO | 1% | 2% | 3% |
| A division's CEO (a company inside a conglomerate) | 0.5% | 0.75% | 1% |

- Only a conglomerate's own CEO can ask for 5%. Before this update a star could ask for 10%.
- A division CEO's stake comes out of the group's holding in that division. When the group grants
  it automatically, it's paid in group shares worth that much.
- The CEO Equity row, the hiring shortlists, the yearly "CEO Wants Ownership" warning and the
  confirmation all show the new amounts.

## 🔒 The shares stay with the company

- A CEO who holds shares still never resigns or retires.
- If they leave anyway (you dismiss or replace them, take over yourself, chair the conglomerate
  again, or they die), their shares go back to you, the founder. For a division, they go back to
  the group's holding in it. Group shares go back to your group stake. The amount you paid for
  the shares comes back with them, so a later sale isn't taxed as an extra gain.
- A CEO who is still in the job keeps their shares.

## Old saves

- CEOs keep any stake they already hold, even one above the new limits.
- Shares still held by CEOs who already left come back to you at the next year-end. Old saves
  don't get any cash.
- A star CEO who hasn't been given shares yet will ask for the new, smaller amount.
