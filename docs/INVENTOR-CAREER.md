# Inventor: from a useful idea to a working product

Find the career under **Activities → Inventor**. Choose the talent before birth or
discover it in childhood. It opens a research career; it no longer promises a
successful invention simply because the player can afford a budget.

## The playable loop

1. From age eight, repair and experiment with borrowed tools for free. Two workshop
   sessions per year build expertise in one of eight fields. Library study and,
   from 1995, online study add topic knowledge and field expertise. Relevant degrees
   and occupations accelerate learning, but a self-taught route remains possible.
2. From sixteen, fund a prepared idea. Small, middle, big and generational projects
   plan for one, two, four and seven research years respectively; a frontier approach
   adds another year. Preparation and infrastructure requirements rise with ambition.
   These are minimum plans, not completion promises.
3. Build a prototype and collect evidence. A practical approach favors cost and
   reliability; balanced work has more commercial upside; frontier work has a wider
   range of outcomes. Engineering experiments and customer trials share one annual
   action slot. A paid specialist improves the design and receives permanent credit.
4. Meet the displayed technical and customer-evidence thresholds, then pass
   independent replication. Failed experiments teach field knowledge but may require
   an explicitly funded redesign. Work stops at an unmet evidence gate. The player
   can take a break or shelve the project, retaining expertise but losing sunk costs.
5. Keep the resulting IP, license manufacturing, or assign it to a compatible company.
   Fabs, staff, working capital, pricing, royalties and territorial patent expiry
   continue to determine commercial results. A patent by itself pays nothing.

There are 38 concrete topics, including practical improvements such as a
repairable fastener, irrigation regulator and assistive grip, plus four private
financial systems. Eight field menus
keep the catalogue manageable. Speculative science is clearly labeled and remains
an alternate-history option with demanding preparation and uncertain results.

## Funding and the person behind the invention

Research grants are competitive, topic-restricted, and expire after two years.
They reduce a project's personal contribution; they never become spendable cash,
taxable personal income, or a refundable balance. Applications are limited to one
per year. Collaborator fees, extra tests and redesigns require explicit spending.
The initial program is prepaid; no surprise annual research bill is charged.

From eighteen, each topic also offers **Company-funded Research · You Lead**.
Choose a compatible company you control and appoint a hired CEO under its
Leadership menu first. The company pays the initial program, collaborator fees,
experiments and redesigns from its own cash, booking them as corporate expenses.
Customer deposits and investment-client assets cannot fund research. You remain
lead inventor and keep attribution; the completed IP belongs to the sponsor and
is automatically installed there. Technical leaders improve engineering progress
and experiments; operations leaders help the project advance. Returning to the
CEO role, losing control or selling the sponsor pauses the project. Shelving
never refunds company expenditure to the player.

## Private financial inventions

**Activities → Inventor → Private financial systems** offers a credit/liquidity
model, execution optimizer, adaptive market model and frontier capital allocation
engine. These use the normal preparation and research loop, with confidential
stress tests and validation on unseen data replacing customer trials. They have
no patent fees, public grants, product manufacturing, external licences or public
breakthrough announcements. Confidential work does not create public fame.

Personally funded methods can be assigned exclusively to one bank or investment
firm you control. Company-funded methods deploy directly to their sponsor. Their
effects improve underwriting, trading research and execution costs inside the
financial simulation; no income is created merely by finishing an invention.
Team skill and morale affect deployment. The edge fades as data becomes stale;
**Financial Operations** offers paid annual revalidation using company cash.
Trading still has capital-capacity limits and losses remain possible.

A balanced schedule advances one funded research year annually. Intensive work is
35% faster but adds substantial strain, especially alongside a job. At 80 strain,
burnout forces a recovery year and affects health and happiness. A voluntary break
reduces strain by 25 annually. Prison pauses research.

Mentors, technical-society demonstrations, paid consulting, disputed credit and
unproven invention promoters create personal choices. Consulting pays taxable fees
only after the player accepts and performs the work. Opportunities are dated,
owner-scoped and resolved once. Ignoring an invitation does not charge a fee.

Completed prototypes earn modest professional credibility. Meaningful adoption
milestones earn stronger reputation and some fame. Creator and collaborator credit
survive assignment to a company. Product sales and actual personal royalties are
recorded separately, so corporate revenue is never mistaken for personal income.
The same completed idea cannot be renamed to restart its patent clock; improve its
existing company product or research another topic.

## Commercial adoption and existing saves

New research products begin with a small early-adopter market. Adoption responds to
prior-year units actually sold, alongside a limited awareness effect. Closing-year
sales cannot boost another partner's demand during that same settlement. The record
survives inheritance, while a parent's expertise, reputation and pending decisions
do not become the child's personal achievements.

New prototypes do not automatically multiply global stock prices. Their economic
contribution comes through actual deployment and the existing economic activity
system. Strategic pressure, licensing, company upgrades and patent competition
remain available.

Previously completed inventions keep their commercial model and rights. Previously
funded projects keep their original paid timeline and completion commitment. The
new research rules apply to newly started projects. Readers normalize saved state
without changing it during menu rendering.

## Design basis and limits

The separation between invention, customer discovery and commercialization follows
[NSF I-Corps](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/initiatives/i-corps) and
[WIPO's commercialization overview](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/ip-commercialization).
The emphasis on technical, IP and market evidence also draws on
[WIPO's lab-to-market guidance](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/technology-transfer/access-market).

Durations, probabilities, costs, expertise thresholds and infrastructure dates are
game-balance choices. Patents still use the game's simplified national filing and
grant process; this change does not model a full patent examination, clinical trial
or employment-IP legal system. The futuristic catalogue is intentionally speculative.

## Verification

Focused tests exercise a successful research playthrough, failed replication and
recovery, grants and money conservation, burnout, collaboration, professional events,
era and preparation gates, pure menus, save/load determinism, inheritance, licensing,
company assignment, adoption timing and settlement replay protection. Existing
invention, business, talent, finance and performance regressions are also covered.
