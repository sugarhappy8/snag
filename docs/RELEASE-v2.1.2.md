# AnotherLife v2.1.2 — Family Plans, Private Finance Research & Leadership

## Highlights

- Married partners can raise starting a family as a choice. Accepted plans persist
  across attempts, conception odds are improved for willing couples, and players
  can ask to try up to 24 times per year.
- Inventors can research four financial systems: private risk models, execution
  optimizers, adaptive market models, and a frontier capital-allocation engine.
  These are internal trade secrets with no patents, public releases, product
  sales, or outside licences. Stale models lose effectiveness and can be revalidated
  using company funds.
- Compatible companies can fund research while the player leads as inventor.
  A hired CEO must run the business. Research, experiments, specialist help and
  redesigns are paid from company cash; the sponsor owns the finished work and
  the player's inventor credit is retained. Company customer funds remain separate.
- Leadership team skill and morale now have stronger, distinct effects on sales,
  capacity, costs, quality, financial risk, growth, and research. Existing elite
  CEO and Business talent advantages remain in place.

See [INVENTOR-CAREER.md](INVENTOR-CAREER.md) for research and funding details.
