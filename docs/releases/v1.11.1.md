# AnotherLife v1.11.1 — Royal Family Careers

Royal relatives now have country- and era-appropriate careers instead of ordinary random jobs.

- UAE roles include ministers, ministry advisers and undersecretaries, emirate officials,
  diwan advisers, university lecturers, qualified deans, university council chairs and
  charitable foundation chairs.
- Historical boundaries keep federal ministries, domestic university roles and female cabinet
  appointments out of earlier UAE lives. Other monarchies use appropriate court, public-duty,
  cultural and charitable roles.
- Both royal parents and eligible siblings/descendants have persistent career paths. Senior
  appointments require age, qualifications and experience; students earn degrees over time.
- Jobs, employers, income, promotions and retirement remain consistent through annual updates.
  Explicit player-company jobs take precedence. Private company ownership does not duplicate
  official salary, and abolition ends official appointments and pay.
- Existing saves upgrade current royal-family profiles without granting money or degrees.
  Newborn saves also correct parent descriptions; older diary entries are preserved.
