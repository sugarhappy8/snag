# AnotherLife v1.11.0 — Business & Dynasties

## What changed

- Banking policy sliders and live previews, enforced capital/liquidity requirements, funded
  budgets, defaults, bank runs and repayable emergency support.
- Retail, private/quant and brokerage investment firms with segregated client assets, research
  and computing budgets, historical infrastructure, strategy capacity and decay.
- Flexible startup capital, rescue funding, and distinct secondary share purchases, new-equity
  subscriptions and company-funded buybacks. Financing is not operating revenue.
- Stronger elite CEO planning and performance explanations; capacity-limited domestic group
  service contracts with consolidated internal-invoice elimination.
- Persistent NPC education, careers, finances, businesses, recruitment, investment/acquisition
  interactions and history across generations.
- Eight randomized family-wealth backgrounds backed by parental resources. Wealthy and
  billionaire births cannot be selected.
- Historically gated royalty, duties and succession. **New Life → Custom Life → Royal birth**
  now lets players choose Random, Royal family or Non-royal family. Rank and succession remain
  subject to dynastic rules; ordinary family wealth stays random.
- Exact numeric entry alongside sliders, mobile previews, and inheritance/qualification fixes.

## Verification

The overhaul's final local sweep exercised 150 files: 2,490 tests passed and two long simulations
exceeded the default 15-second timeout. Both unchanged assertions passed after assigning those
tests explicit longer limits, accounting for all 2,492 tests in that batch. The subsequent
royal-birth selector passed 120 relevant tests across six files, including 15 new checks.
TypeScript validation, production builds, financial-preview/founding browser checks and an
actual selected royal birth passed. Remote CI is skipped as requested; deployment only builds
and publishes the already locally verified game.

## Known limits

Royal characters are fictional branches of historical dynasties; banking regulations are game
approximations. Internal contracts currently require wholly controlled domestic divisions.
NPC private equity has no secondary resale screen. The build retains a roughly 7.03 MB
minified JavaScript bundle (2.38 MB gzip), with a bundle-size warning.
