# AnotherLife v4.0.1

Three fixes from player reports. Saves from v4.0.0 load unchanged.

## Fixes

- **A company you own showed twice on your CV.** A founder who was still running or had stepped
  back from their own company got one row for the company and another for the years as CEO. Now
  each company is one row, with the years you were CEO folded into it ("91 employees, 8 years as
  CEO"). A sold or closed company keeps its single historic row, and two different companies with
  the same name stay two rows.
- **A PhD straight after a bachelor's degree.** The game let a life in any country start a PhD
  with only a bachelor's. A PhD now needs a master's degree first, except in the countries where
  a direct route is the norm: the United States, Puerto Rico, Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland,
  Australia, Hong Kong and Singapore. A refusal says what is missing ("A PhD in India normally
  needs a master's degree first."), the Graduate School menu and the "What's next?" prompt
  after graduation follow the same rule, and a refused application doesn't use up your yearly
  application. Lives already in a PhD keep going.
- **Game studios could make trillions.** A hit game's back catalog grew by more than the original
  sales every year, so one title could compound without limit, and nothing tied sales to the
  number of players in the world. A hit's tail can no longer feed its own growth, a single title
  can't sell more than a share of that era's gamers over its life, and a publisher (including
  every studio you own together) can't sell more than a share of the world's gamers in a year.
  The best studios now top out in the low tens of billions a year, as real industry leaders do.
  Ordinary studios are unchanged.

## Not in this release

The suggestions to declare war as a president and for better profit and loss charts and rankings
are not built.
