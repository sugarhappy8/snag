# AnotherLife v2.1.3 — Living Dynasties

## Highlights

- Children of Main Character Syndrome parents always inherit the talent, including existing children in saved games.
- Conglomerates remain intact through succession: children inherit ownership shares while divisions, treasury, and management continue operating.
- Gifted NPC children use their talent in education, careers, and businesses. Their ambition can drive independently funded companies and direct rivals.
- Family co-owner shares are preserved across dividends, dilution, buybacks, spin-offs, and future succession.
- Known family profiles show ambition and company ownership wealth.

See [Dynasty mechanics](DYNASTY.md) for details. Autonomous NPC startups currently exclude game studios and deep-tech labs, which require separate launch and research workflows.
