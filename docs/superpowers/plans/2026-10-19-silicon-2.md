**Goal:** A chip company prices its chip lines (graphics, CPUs, memory, accelerators) against one market price, earns slow, capped goodwill by selling cheaper than the market during the 2024-2027 chip squeeze, and can hand a pricing policy to a hired CEO.

**Architecture:** One calm reference (`marketReference`) and one index (`priceIndex`) next to `techPlan`. Lanes are discounts below the headline price, blended inside `techPlan` after `marketCtxFor` (`buildCtx` and its cache are untouched). The yearly sim books revenue as `sold * plan.mix` and runs `bookSilicon` (goodwill); `afterDeepTechYearImpl` applies the brand step, a seeded rival reaction and the chip-leap news. Goodwill acts only through `b.brand`. Pure rules live in `silicon-data.ts`, `silicon.ts` and `silicon-text.ts`.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript (strict), Vitest, React (`AnnualReport.tsx` only). No new dependency.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-19-silicon-2-design.md` (section numbers such as 4.4 refer to it). Where "Decisions" below differ from the spec, the plan wins.

**Decisions this plan makes beyond the spec**
- The spec's build rows 4 and 5 are two tasks each here (3 and 4; 5 and 6) so a reviewer can reject one half. Row 0 is the Preflight block.
- `ChipLane` gains `noun` (the slider label). The `core` row's `weight` is 0.25, the least core ever keeps; its live weight is 1 minus the open extras. `core` is never stored in `silicon.lanes` (it sells at the headline), so at most 4 ids are stored.
- `planContribution(g, b, plan, year = g.year)` takes the year. `bestTechPlan` clamps each candidate to `limit` (it does not skip it) and returns `limit` already rounded to 2 places; the cap "clipped" the plan when `price >= limit`.
- `SiliconYear.step` is `brandStep(goodwill after the year)`. The brand step is applied once, behind `applied`, through `pendingBrandStep(t)`.
- `stackMod` ignores an expired mod. The two new log lines go through `log()` (it takes an id from `g.nextId`); no `rand`, `chance` or direct `uid` call is added.
- The squeeze sentence is part of the Goodwill row, not a row of its own: that keeps Chips & Fabs at 14 rows at most.
- Leap: announce when the chips beat every rival (at least 2 rivals) and `year - lead > 3` (or `lead` is 0); while the lead continues, `lead` just advances.

## Review Focus

Failure modes the spec implies but its own test list does not name, most likely first. Each is pinned in the task shown.

1. **A chip lab that sells nothing in a squeeze year** (no capacity, zero production, demand 0): goodwill relaxes 12% of the gap toward 50, nothing is NaN, no rival cuts prices, no log line. (Task 3, Task 4)
2. **A discount saved on a lane that isn't open** (old or hostile save, a fab that is gone): price, demand and books ignore it, the row is hidden and pressing the action does nothing. (Task 2, Task 5)
3. **The headline moves mid-year** (price war, offensive, event, price promise): every lane follows by the same fraction, no lane prices above the headline, revenue stays `sold * mix`. (Task 2, Task 3)
4. **A pricing cap below anything the profit search would choose** (low brand and quality, cap 60%): the plan is clamped to the cap, never under one cent, never NaN, and an owner who types a higher price in the Annual Report isn't stopped. (Task 2, Task 5)
5. **A save made mid-window or after it** (a 2026 lab with no `silicon`, an heir, a booking in 2028): goodwill starts at 50 and moves from the first booked squeeze year, decays toward 50 after 2027, a second booking changes nothing, and the rival reaction repeats the same way. (Task 3, Task 4)
