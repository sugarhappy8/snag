## 1. Track summary

**Items covered (16 tasks).** D.3 (the Edge, about 40 memory kinds, the `addRelationship` shim, the `WealthTies.owes` and `Wl.anger` migration), D.4 (the SocialAct engine with its ledger lines, the feud ladder, staff loyalty), D.12 (determinism, money scan, 120-year probe, save size, migration matrix) and the golden tests of relationships, love, wealth, will and nepo that must land before the shim (R4-E1 to R4-E4). The roadmap folds those goldens into D.12's 700 lines; they are about 3,400 test lines (see "Line budget").

**The design in one paragraph.** `Person.relationship` stays the authority through R4. Every write goes through `addRelationship(g, p, delta, kind?)` or `setRelationship(g, p, value, kind?)` in `core.ts`, whose body is the old expression verbatim; the change it applied (`before`, `after`, `kind`) goes through a kernel port (`social.mirror`, because `core.ts` is layer 0 and cannot import `world/`) to `world/edge-mirror.ts`, which keeps the Edge `person>player` in step: affection equals `Person.relationship` exactly, an `offence` also raises resentment, a `decay` (the yearly drift) does not. Edges live in `g.world.edges`, the first production use of an entity-sharded slice (a tap rewrites one shard, not the world chunk), keyed `'<ref>><ref>'` (the first end feels about the second), bounded at 1,200 edges and 8 memories each (40 kinds); the Registry track's `met` shard wants the same plumbing, so E6 lands the slice-row pattern and they copy it (open question 9). A tiny `g.world.social` shard is the stamp: `createNewLife` writes it only when the build flag `social` is on, and every new behaviour (the mirror, the memory odds terms, the acts, the shared popup budget) runs only on a stamped life. So every v5.2.0 save, every existing test and every unstamped life stay byte-identical, golden digests included, until R4-E16 flips the flag and re-records once. The old records keep their jobs: `WealthTies.owes` and `Wl.anger` are mirrored into edge debt, obligation and resentment at their two writers and by a pure overlay on read (a write on first read would break menu purity), and no existing reader moves. The SocialAct engine splits a pure `plan` (preconditions, a consequence patch, ledger lines, luck from `outcomeStream(g, 'social', ...)`) from one applier that books money through `cash.moveCash`, writes edges and emits the log and popups. Money between people reuses `support`; debts live on the edge (`dv`), so a divorce deficiency or a loan is one record in one place.
