## 1. Track summary

**The design in one paragraph.** `Person.relationship` stays the authority through R4 (X22). Every write goes through `addRelationship(g, p, delta, kind?)` or `setRelationship(g, p, value, kind?)` in `core.ts`, whose body is the old expression verbatim; the change it applied (`before`, `after`, `kind`) goes to a hook in `kernel/relations.ts` (`bindMirror`; `core.ts` is layer 0 and cannot import `world/`, and a port would stay unbound for three tasks, C3, X23) that `world/edge-mirror.ts` binds at load. The mirror keeps the Edge `person>player` in step: affection equals `Person.relationship` exactly, an `offence` also raises resentment, a `decay` (the yearly drift, the nepo mood steps) does not. Edges live in `g.world.edges`, the first production entity-sharded slice after the Registry's S5 spike (a tap rewrites one shard of eight, not the world chunk), keyed `'<ref>><ref>'` (the first end feels about the second), bounded at 1,200 edges and 8 memories each (40 kinds). A tiny `g.world.social` shard is the stamp: `createNewLife` writes it only when the build flag `social` is on (P3 adds the key, E16 flips it with the version bump), and every new behaviour (the mirror, the memory odds terms, the acts, the shared popup budget) runs only on a stamped life (X6). So every v5.2.0 save, every existing test and every unstamped life stay byte-identical, golden digests included, until E16 flips the flag and re-records once. The old records keep their jobs: `WealthTies.owes` and `Wl.anger` are mirrored into edge debt, obligation and resentment at their two writers and by a pure overlay on read (a write on first read would break menu purity), and no existing reader moves. The SocialAct engine splits a pure `plan` (preconditions, a consequence patch, ledger lines, luck from `outcomeStream(g, 'social', ...)`) from one applier (`runAct`) that books money through `cash.moveCash`, writes edges and emits the log and popups. Money between people reuses `support` (a divorce uses `divorce`); debts live on the edge (`dv`), so a loan and Life's divorce deficiency are one record in one place (X24). What R5 and R6 read from this track is a section 3.1 contract and is frozen at its merge.
