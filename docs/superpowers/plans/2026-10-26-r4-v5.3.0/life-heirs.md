## 1. Track summary

**Items covered (12 tasks).** D.5 life stages: R4-L2 to R4-L5. D.9 heirs and dynasty carryover: R4-L9 to R4-L11. A.K8 the heir carryover of world state, decision D4: R4-L6 to R4-L8. The pins that must exist before the shim: R4-L1. Acceptance, ceilings, docs: R4-L12.

**The design in one paragraph.** Everything new runs only on a stamped life (`socialOn(g)`, the Edges track's stamp; see lead action 2), so an unstamped life, every v5.2.0 save and every golden stay byte-identical and the track is dark by construction until the lead flips the build key at R4-E16. The legacy fields stay authoritative. An NPC's `married`, `spouseName` and `kids` are written on 4 lines and read on about a dozen, and one reader moves money (`npc-lives.ts:369`), so the records MIRROR them the way the Edge mirrors `Person.relationship`: when a relative marries or has a baby, the same name, gender and year become a Met record (identity stored once, so the face never changes), linked from the relative's `Person.data` (`spouseSid`, `kidSids`) and by structural edges. Records are not stepped. `ageTo` (pure, from the family seed and the existing `npcMortality` table) says whether a record is alive in any year; a yearly step in the `life` phase (order 37, just before `relationships`) applies only the transitions that touch other state (a widowing, a divorce unlink, a fact for the news). When a relative dies, a second step (`life` phase, order 55, after `relationships` and `sharedProjects`) moves the cash left on the dead record and the stakes to the spouse and children through the ledger, materializing them as Persons only then. `continueAsChild` keeps its draws in the same order; a stamped hand-over blends each re-rolled kin relationship with what the family felt, keeps every bound person, copies edges through `heirEdges` (trust, respect and a non-kin's affection at 0.5, resentment at 0.6; a direct kin's affection is their blended relationship, so it is not half of the old number: X33), brings the NPC spouse over with the same face, pays what the player owed people before the slices, passes what people owed the player to the heir by the will's share, and turns a divorce deficiency into an obligation paid over years. Cut-out and shorted relatives quarrel with the favored ones on NPC-NPC edges.
