## 1. Track summary

**Items covered (12 tasks).** D.5 life stages: R4-L2 to R4-L5. D.9 heirs and dynasty carryover: R4-L9 to R4-L11. A.K8 the heir carryover of world state, decision D4: R4-L6 to R4-L8. The pins that must exist before the shim: R4-L1. Acceptance, ceilings, docs: R4-L12.

**The design in one paragraph.** Everything new runs only on a stamped life (`socialOn(g)`, E6b's stamp; see lead action 2), so an unstamped life, every v5.2.0 save and every golden stay byte-identical and the track is dark by construction until the lead flips the build key in R4-E16 (one commit with the 5.3.0 bump, after the exit-gate walk). The legacy fields stay authoritative (X22). An NPC's `married`, `spouseName` and `kids` are written on 4 lines and read on about a dozen, and one reader moves money (`npc-lives.ts:369`), so the records MIRROR them the way the Edge mirrors `Person.relationship`: when a relative marries or has a baby, the same name, gender and year become a Met record (identity stored once, so the face never changes), linked from the relative's `Person.data` (`spouseSid`, `kidSids`) and by structural edges. Records are not stepped. `ageTo` (pure, from the family seed and the existing `npcMortality` table) says whether a record is alive in any year; a yearly step in the `life` phase (order 37, just before `relationships`) applies only the transitions that touch other state (a widowing, a divorce unlink, a fact for the news). When a relative dies, a second step (`life` phase, order 55, after `relationships` and `sharedProjects`) moves the cash left on the dead record and the stakes to the spouse and children through the ledger, materializing them as Persons only then. `continueAsChild` keeps its draws in the same order; a stamped hand-over blends each re-rolled kin relationship with what the family felt, keeps every bound person, copies edges through `heirEdges` (trust, respect and a non-kin's affection at 0.5, resentment at 0.6; a direct kin's affection is their blended relationship, so it is not half of the old number: X33), brings the NPC spouse over with the same face, pays what the player owed people before the slices, passes what people owed the player to the heir by the will's share, and turns a divorce deficiency into an obligation paid over years. Cut-out and shorted relatives quarrel with the favored ones on NPC-NPC edges.

- The registry, persona, the edge model, the `addRelationship` shim, the acts, the feud ladder and secrets are the other three tracks'. - Lazy school, job and move for records (design 7.5): they need the jobs shard (A.K3, R6). A record has an age, a spouse, children, an illness and a death.
- Classmates, coworkers, dating candidates, strangers and the other named-person shapes (X11): R6 D.6. The hand-over drops them unless bound.
- Houses and cars of non-heir siblings, and a sibling who already owns a firm: `npcCompany` is one record per person and an NPC has no home entity (`NpcLife.housing` is a number), so these stay cash (X35).
- Heir liability for an insolvent estate (decision D4(b) read as: no, X24). Person debts do not enter `netWorth` (`core.ts:468`).
- Reputation, gossip, the composer: R5. The UI track reads `data.kin` through `relationLabel` (U9 owns it; `core.ts:845` ignores it today, C19, X38) and `data.spouseName`; `kinView` has no UI reader in R4. Cousins and the popup in-laws of the player's own spouse (`events/wealth-family.ts:48-57`, `inLaw()` and `cousin()` build a name per popup) stay per-popup names: routing them through `touchMet` would change their draw order and move every seeded wealth popup (X46). ## 5. Risks

1. **Two code paths.** Every change is stamped-only, so the legacy path lives as long as old saves do. Both are tested; deleting the legacy path is a later release.
2. **Mirror drift.** Two sources of truth (`married`, `spouseName`, `kids` and the records). `kinProblems` and the yearly sync hold them; a writer that clears `married` is caught a year late.
3. **The blend.** Averaging the old relationship with the legacy roll keeps the dice and a sane start, but it is not "half strength"; it is an owner decision with its default taken (X33).
4. **Hand-over shape.** `continueAsChild` is one 560-line function edited by five hunks of this track and by R4-E5's codemod; L6 and L9 both edit `life.ts:1496-1497` and L9 waits for L6, and the calls land in the order L6, L9, L11; the whole track merges after E5.
5. **Materialized kin grow `g.people`.** At most 12 a year and 30 alive, as 'friend' with `data.kin`; they may ask for money through the wealth asks (inside their caps). `cleanupPeople` does not trim friends today.
6. **Facts are first come.** 8 a year into a store of 400 rows that drops the oldest; R5 detectors share it.
7. **Interfaces not in code.** Everything from R4-S* and R4-E* is a plan; `touchMet`'s identity argument, `markMet`'s set semantics, `serviceDebt`, `debtToken` and the `dv` kind code are accepted (X12, X24) and frozen by cross-check section 3.1, but a task re-reads the real signature when its prerequisites have merged and stops with the lead if one differs.
8. **Layering.** `modules/heirs.ts` and `modules/kin-estates.ts` sit at layer 4 so `life.ts` can call them; `kin.ts`, `divorce-debt.ts` and `will-strife.ts` import only their own domain, layers 0 to 2 and `society/contract.ts`. A slip shows as a domain-pair increase in `layers.test.ts`, and this track never edits `test/layers-baseline.json`.
9. **Repetition gates bite whenever lives diverge.** Stamped lives diverge by one uid per kin line; the new lines are rare and name people, but the flip (E16) re-runs the three gates and the rehearsal runs them first.
10. **Opening money of a materialized spouse** is an exempt literal; a ceiling row bounds it at 40 times typical income (UNVERIFIED constant).
11. **Time cost.** `syncKin` is incremental; `settleKinEstates` and the hand-over loops are bounded by caps. Measured: a full `ageTo` scan is 22 microseconds. The year-step case at the caps (1,200 edges, 400 Met, 160 kin records) is E15's.
