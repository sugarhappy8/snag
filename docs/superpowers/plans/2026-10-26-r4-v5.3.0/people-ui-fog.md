## 1. Track summary

**Items covered (11 tasks).** H.7 and D.10 share the person profile (U2 to U5, U9, U10), H.5 is hired staff and the CEO (U6, U7), G.10a is the People cockpit (U8), U1 is the Exact oracle and U11 the acceptance. **The design in one paragraph.** Knowledge (R3) already turns a hidden number into an estimate with a band and a chip, and the Fog track used it on markets and on the staff shortlist; nothing yet fogs a person. This track makes the person screen say what the player can see. A pure builder `personFacts(g, p)` returns rows that are `known` (name, age, relation, job title, your own Relationship bar, notes), `estimate` (happiness, health, smarts, looks, cash, an NPC's skill and net worth, each a word or a range with the confidence chip) or `unknown` (a trait not yet seen, "Private" cash, "N more things you can't tell yet"). How sharp an estimate is comes from **exposure**, a pure number from `yearsKnown`, `relationship` and `livesWithPlayer` that Knowledge's `exp` option already accepts and no screen passes (`estimate.ts:161-162`). It needs no saved field, and an heir gets the exposure their re-rolled relationships earn (`life.ts:1370-1377`), so nothing is inherited. Eight new rule rows give observable things (looks) a tighter passive sd than inner ones (smarts, traits). One owner file `people-fog.ts` registers the `ppl` key kind, so "Look closer" can sell a private investigator's read of a person (the one source that reads `ppl`, `sources.ts:69`). Hired key staff and CEOs get the same treatment with tenure as exposure: the skill reads as a range that tightens, the trait is named after a year or a background check, and the "current contribution" sentence is recomputed on the player's read so it stops leaking the true skill by inversion. The People cockpit gets four tiles and two new badges over facts the player already has. Everything is presentation: the simulation never reads the mode, the new layout sits behind one build flag `peopleProfile` and Realistic only (P3 adds the key, off by default; E16 turns it on with the version bump unless the lead's walk fails, X8), Exact and flag-off are byte-identical to v5.2.0 (recorded first, U1), and no life, save or golden digest moves. The profile cards (`FactRow`, `PersonProfile` and the builders only they import) load as a lazy chunk from U4, so they stay out of the first download (X48).
