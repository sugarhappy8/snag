# R&D 3.0: operations R&D, patents, credits, a research team and rivals that do R&D

Status: design for the plan, written 2026-10-19. Path: architectural.
Base: main v3.0.0 without release 1 (tech waves), which is built on another branch and edits `heavy-rd.ts`
(`advanceHeavy`), `state.ts` (`BizState.wave`) and `yearly.ts`. This slice never edits `advanceHeavy` and keeps its
edits to those three files to small hunks of their own. No release notes and no version bump: both happen at integration.

Names marked "(new)" don't exist yet. Every other name below was grepped in main v3.0.0 (see the last line).

## 1. What the player asked for

> more R&D for all companies: low-tech, mid-tech, high-tech and breakthroughs.

Today a company has an ordinary product line, a rare Heavy R&D breakthrough, and (deep tech only) a lab. Missing is the
R&D a normal company does between those: process and cost work, patents, a tax credit, a research team, and rivals that
improve their own products. Success looks like this in one play session:

1. Open a mid-tech company (a brewery). Under Run the Company a Research & Operations screen sits after Products.
2. Start "Lean Production System": a fixed price, years, odds and a ceiling on what it can do. A business-talented owner
   sees a purple hint that comes from the same math the year-end uses.
3. The annual report has its own lines for the R&D tax credit, a research grant and the team's pay. Unit costs are lower,
   and the effect fades unless the company keeps working.
4. Patent a first-of-its-kind line: rivals can't sell one for a fixed number of years. Or license it and collect royalties.
5. Hire a scientist: every R&D project's odds and the team's slots rise a little. Over ten years rivals' versions of each
   kind improve, so a line has to be renewed.

Assumptions (mine, not the player's words):

- "Low, mid, high, breakthrough" is a per-trade tier table (`RD_TIER`, new). Breakthrough is the 12 deep-tech trades.
- "All companies" means every company with a Products screen except deep-tech, finance and studio companies (the gate
  Heavy R&D uses, `heavyEligible`) for Operations R&D, patents, the credit and the team. Rival R&D reaches every market, deep tech's Products menu too.
- Every price is fixed per trade and program. Nothing scales with the company's cash, sales or valuation (spec
  2026-10-12 section 1: "R&D must not scale with how much money the company has").

## 2. What the audit found (v3.0.0)

- **Two R&D kinds and a lab.** Ordinary lines: `rdCost`, `rdQuote`, `startRd`, `advanceRd` in `rd.ts`. Heavy R&D for 39 trades:
  `heavyEligible`, `heavyOffers`, `startHeavy`, `advanceHeavy` in `heavy-rd.ts`, five entries per trade in `heavy-data.ts`,
  menu `heavyNodes` and `heavyAction` in `manage.ts`. Deep tech: 111 lab programs (`PROGRAMS`, `deeptech-data.ts`) plus the
  small Products menu. Finance and studios have no R&D family (hedge algorithm in `fund-actions.ts`, bank `PRODUCTS` in
  `bank-state.ts`, `gamestudio-engines.ts`).
- **Ordinary R&D changes demand pull and nothing else.** `launchP` reads CEO skill, quality and `staffPower(b, 'quality')`;
  `rdSlots` is 1 + floor(log2(1 + sites)). No durable effect on unit cost, capacity or demand exists.
- **The effect-bearing levers aren't R&D.** `STRATEGIES` has an 'efficiency' card (cost -0.08, `dynamics.ts`);
  `businessEffects` clamps demand .7-1.65, cost .65-1.5, capacity at 1.4; the automation level of
  `purchaseEquipmentUpgrade` (`supply-policy.ts`, cap .35, fades x.97) only feeds shortage recovery (`supplyConditions`,
  `supply.ts`); `CAPACITY_LINES` covers 8 trades.
- **No patent, exclusivity or royalty for ordinary companies.** `Product` and `RdProject` (`state.ts`) have no such field,
  `HeavyRecord.by` only names the pioneer, `heavyArrival` lets rivals follow for free, and `valuationOf` (`economy.ts`) has
  no classic IP term (deep tech has `deepTechValue`). The v2.7.0 notes defer patents. The Workshop's patents are personal
  (`patentLaw` in `workshop/market.ts`: term, fee, enforcement by country).
- **Credit and grants are deep tech only.** `RD_CREDIT` (30 countries) and `GRANTS` (`GrantDef.who` is
  `DeepTechIndustry[] | 'all'`) are read in `simulateDeepTechYearImpl` (`deeptech-sim.ts`). `startRd` and `startHeavy` expense
  the price in full (`b.cash -= n; b.yearCosts += n`).
- **No research team.** `maxKeyStaff` (`staff.ts`) caps key staff and `rdSlots` ignores staff. Quality staff also move the
  quality target (`LEADERSHIP.quality` in `evolve`), so scientists can't be key staff without side effects.
- **Rivals never do R&D.** `versionOf` (`product-market.ts`) is a pure hash of rival id and kind, so a rival's version never
  changes. The player's own line is what ages (`AGE_RATE`, `effectiveQuality`). `heavyArrival` reacts to the player's
  breakthroughs only.
- **The ageing style is missing for 29 of 55 trades.** `STYLE` (`products.ts`) lists 26 ids and `styleOf` returns 'service' for
  the rest, so telecom, data centers, batteries, solar, energy and clinics age at .7 points a year.
- **No tier table.** Groupings exist (`DEEP_TECH_IDS`, `isFinanceFirm`, `isStudio`, `styleOf`, `baseYears`) but no
  low/mid/high/breakthrough. `heavy-data.test.ts` is the template for a totality guard.
- **Heavy has no hint and no hired-CEO path.** `heavyAction` has no `hint`, `productTurn` (`board-ceo.ts`) calls
  `bestRdWithin` only, and `heavyGain` is a floor used by the board vote.
- **Patterns to copy.** `bookFranchiseYear` and `bookHedgeYear` run before `simulateYear` in `yearlyBusinessImpl`
  (`fiscal = g.year - 1`) and move cash once; `bookYear` counts `yearIncome` and `yearCosts` without moving it again
  (`alreadyBooked`). `franchiseIncomeFor` splits a statement line, `franchiseFeesFor` is kept out of `valuationOf`'s earnings,
  `deepTechValue` carries the IP book (`ipBook += min(spent, nominal) x ipShare`, x0.9 a year), `effectiveAutomation` fades a
  level by year, and the Products group hint (`productsMenu`) and `bestRdWithin` share `bestRdIdea`.
- **`fieldOf` has seven call sites:** `rd.ts` (`rdQuote`, `liftPath`), `economy.ts` (`marketCtxFor`), `heavy-rd.ts`
  (`heavyGain`), `product-market.ts` (`fieldFor`, `applyProductRivalry` twice). `advanceRd` draws `g.rng` per finished project.

### 4.2 The lab state (shared by tasks 2 to 5)

One optional field, `BizState.lab?: Json` (new), stored raw and normalized by `readLab` (new, `lab-state.ts`, light imports
like `franchise-state.ts`). Every write goes through `saveLab` (new), which replaces the object, so a reader may cache by
object identity.

```ts
export type LabTrack = 'cost' | 'yield' | 'quality'          // (new types in lab-state.ts)
export interface LabLevel { v: number; at: number }            // v 0-.95, fades x.95 a year from year `at`
export interface LabProgram { id: string; started: number; years: number; progress: number; odds: number; price: number; grant: number }
export interface LabPatent {
  family: string; filed: number; until: number               // protected while year < until
  mode: 'hold' | 'license'; lic: number                      // lic: first licensed year (0: not licensed)
  plan: number                                               // the fixed development price it is based on
  book: number; earned: number                               // IP book value; royalties received so far
}
export interface LabRow { year: number; credit: number; grant: number; royalty: number; payroll: number }
export interface LabState {
  v: 1
  levels: Record<LabTrack, LabLevel>
  runs: Record<LabTrack, number>                             // wins per track: the learning curve (0-12)
  done: Record<string, number>                               // program id -> year of its last win (cooldown)
  programs: LabProgram[]                                     // at most 3, one per track
  team: number[]                                             // year each scientist was hired, at most 6
  patents: LabPatent[]                                       // at most 6
  filed: string[]                                            // families ever patented here, at most 40
  due: { grant: number }                                     // grant earned, paid at the next booking
  booked: number; stepped: number; lastGrant: number         // last fiscal year booked and advanced; year of the last grant
  ledger: LabRow[]                                           // last 4 fiscal years
}
```

`readLab` clamps and drops: unknown program ids, years outside 1800-2300, non-finite or negative money, anything past the
caps above, a patent whose `until <= filed`, a family over 80 characters. A field of the wrong type reads as the empty
state. Other exports of `lab-state.ts` (all new): `opsEffectsOf`, `shieldsOf`, `fieldMarketOf`, `labValue`, `labIncomeIn`, `labPayrollIn`, `labSplit`.
`normalizeBiz` gets one line that keeps `lab` when it is a plain object.

### 4.3 Credit, grants and the ledger (task 2)

`bookLabYear(g, a, b, fiscal)` (new, `lab-year.ts`) is called in `yearlyBusinessImpl` right after `bookHedgeYear`. It returns at
once unless `heavyEligible(b)`, and when `readLab(b).booked >= fiscal` (yearly idempotence). Otherwise:

```
spend   = sum of budgets of b.rd projects and lab programs with started === fiscal        (ordinary, Heavy, Operations)
credit  = round(min(LAB_CREDIT_CAP, spend x clampN(RD_CREDIT[b.countryId] ?? 0, 0, 0.5)))    // the deep-tech math
grant   = due.grant               // earned when a program finished, see 4.4
royalty = sum over licensed patents (4.6)
payroll = team.length x teamPay(b)  // 4.5
```

Credit, grant and royalty are each `b.cash += x; b.yearIncome += x`. Payroll is `b.cash -= x; b.yearCosts += x`. Cash moves
once, here, and `bookYear` counts the amounts without moving it again. The year's `LabRow` is written, `due` is cleared and
`booked = fiscal` is set. Nothing is created when every amount is zero and no `lab` exists.

- `LAB_CREDIT_CAP` (new) is 250,000 (2026 dollars), fixed. Scientists' pay isn't counted as spend (no pump by hiring).
- A flop is not clawed back. The credit is at most 50% of the spend, so failing never makes money.
- **Grants** (`LAB_GRANTS`, new): `where`, `tiers`, `share`, `max`. us .25 max 60K (mid, high); gb .30 50K (all three); de .30
  120K (mid, high); fr .30 80K (all); jp .25 60K (low, mid); ca .30 70K (all); au .25 50K (all); sg .35 80K (all); kr .25 70K
  (mid, high); any other country .10 20K (all). A grant applies to an Operations program only, to a company with at most 25
  sites (`totalOutlets`), and only when `lastGrant` is 3 or more years old. At start the amount is
  `min(max, price x min(share, 0.5 - creditRate))`, stored in the program and paid after the work is done, win or flop: a grant
  covers R&D and never turns it into profit. `lastGrant` is set at start, so two programs can't both claim.
- **Ledger.** `statementLines` (`yearly.ts`) takes the lab's amounts out of "Other income" and the payroll out of "One-off
  costs" and shows "R&D tax credit and grants", "Patent licences" and "Research team", the way `franchised` is split out.
- **Valuation.** `valuationOf` subtracts `labIncomeIn(b, r.year)` (credit, grant, royalty), after tax, from its profit average
  like `franchiseFeesFor`, so a refund or royalty is never capitalized. It adds `labValue(b)`, the sum of patent `book` (4.6).

### 4.4 Operations R&D (task 3)

Three tracks, rungs 1 to 3: `cost` (unit cost), `yield` (output per site), `quality` (a quality system that cuts returns and
wins repeat customers, so it enters through demand). Low tier offers rungs 1 and 2; mid and high 1 to 3. Names are the same in
every trade.

| Id (new) | Label | First year |
|---|---|---|
| ops.cost.1 / .2 / .3 | Time-and-Motion Study / Lean Production System / Computerized Planning | 1905 / 1955 / 1985 |
| ops.yield.1 / .2 / .3 | Layout Redesign / Process Automation / Robotics and Sensors | 1905 / 1960 / 1990 |
| ops.quality.1 / .2 / .3 | Inspection Checklists / Statistical Process Control / Automated Quality Tracking | 1905 / 1950 / 1995 |

**Fixed price, years, odds** (`opsPrice`, `opsYears`, `opsOdds`, new, `lab-rules.ts`):

```
opsBase(b) = roundBudget(RD_BASE x rdSiteScale(b))       // rdSiteScale (new, rd.ts) is rdCost's site-sales term, extracted; rdCost is unchanged
price      = roundBudget(opsBase x RUNG_X[rung])            RUNG_X = [1, 2.2, 4.5]
years      = clamp(ceil(baseYears(b.industry) x RUNG_YEARS[rung]), 1, 4)        RUNG_YEARS = [1, 1.5, 2]
odds       = clamp(0.9 x launchP(g, b) + OPS_TIER[tier].odds - RUNG_PENALTY[rung], .15, .92)       RUNG_PENALTY = [0, .05, .10]
```

By arithmetic from the audit (not run): a restaurant's `opsBase` is about $57K, so its rungs cost about $57K, $130K and $260K; a
manufacturer's base is about $150K. Cash, sales and valuation appear nowhere in a price.

**Effect channels.** A track's level is `v x .95^(year - at)`. `opsEffectsOf(b, year)` returns `{ cost: -capCost x L_cost, capacity:
capYield x L_yield, demand: capQuality x L_quality }` with the caps of `OPS_TIER` (new):

| Tier | odds add | cost cap | yield cap | quality cap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| low | .25 | .05 | .06 | .04 |
| mid | .20 | .08 | .10 | .05 |
| high | .12 | .10 | .10 | .07 |

`businessEffects` (`dynamics.ts`) adds these before its existing clamps, for all of the company's countries, and is
byte-identical when there is no `lab`. They aren't multiplied by `policyMarket`. The old clamps (cost .65-1.5, capacity 1.4,
demand .7-1.65) stay the outer bound.

**Learning curve.** On a win at fiscal year `F`: `gain = (1 - Leff) x RUNG_GAIN[rung] x 0.8^runs[track]`, `RUNG_GAIN = [.30, .40, .50]`,
`Leff` the track's level in year `F + 1`. Then `v = Leff + gain`, `at = F + 1`, `runs[track] += 1` (cap 12), `done[id] = F`. Each win on
a track is worth 20% less than the last, and the level fades 5% a year, so the ceiling is a cost to keep.

**Rules.** One program per track at a time. A program can't run again until 3 years after its last win. A flop loses the price,
adds no level and leaves the program available. A start needs the price plus `rdReserve(b)` in cash. `opsBlock(g, b, id, cashPaid?)`
(new) returns one reason or null, in this order: not eligible, unknown id, era (`g.year < first` or `!eraFits`), rung not offered
at this tier, track busy, cooldown, storage guard (`MAX_STORED_PROJECTS`), cash (skipped when `cashPaid`, which only the hired
CEO sets, because the board budget has already taken the price).

**Start and roll.** `startOps(g, a, b, id, pay)` (new) re-checks `opsBlock`, takes the price (`b.cash`, `b.yearCosts`) when `pay` is true, works out
the grant and pushes a `LabProgram`, with no random draw. `advanceLab(g, b, year)` (new) runs at the top of `advanceRd`, after the year is booked, guarded
by `stepped`. It adds a year of progress to each program and rolls `chance(g, odds)` once per finished program in track order (cost,
yield, quality): the only draw in the whole slice. Win or flop, the program's `grant` moves into `due`.

### 4.5 Research team (task 4)

```
teamCap  = { low: 2, mid: 4, high: 6 }[tier]           // breakthrough: never offered
teamPay  = roundBudget(2.2 x localEcon(industry, country, city).wage)      // per scientist a year, fixed per trade
hire fee = 0.5 x teamPay at hire; release only after 3 years' service, fee 0.5 x teamPay     (b.cash, b.yearCosts)
power    = sum over i < n of 0.7^i                     // 1, 1.7, 2.19, 2.53, 2.77, 2.94
odds     = + 0.03 x power inside launchP, before its clamp (at most +.088)
slots    = + min(2, floor(n / 2)) in rdSlots
```

Both edits are additive in `rd.ts` and read `b.lab` directly. Operations odds use `launchP` too, so the team lifts them. `teamValue(g, b)` (new) is the change in the best ordinary idea's value
(`bestRdIdea`) plus the change in the best Operations program's `opsValue`, each recomputed with n + 1 scientists, less a year's pay. `teamBlock`
(new) gives one reason: not eligible, cap reached, cash (fee + a year's pay + `rdReserve`), or under 3 years' service (release). A
project keeps the odds it started with, so hire-start-release gains nothing the minimum service doesn't charge for. Payroll is
booked in `bookLabYear` and shown on the statement. A scientist isn't key staff, so the key-staff cap and the quality target
aren't touched.

### 4.6 Patents and licensing (task 5)

**Who can patent a family** (`patentBlock(g, b, family, cashPaid?)`, new; one reason or null, the cash test skipped when `cashPaid`):

- `heavyEligible(b)`; fewer than 6 live patents; the family isn't in `lab.filed` (once per family, no refiling).
- The company sells a line of that family it developed itself (no `from`), launched at most 3 years ago.
- Low tier: only a Heavy breakthrough family (`breakthroughByName`); a recipe or a service isn't patentable. Mid and high: any family.
- Novelty: `fieldFor(ctx).of(family).offer <= 0.02`, the test the Products rows use for "no rival sells one yet".
- Cash: the fee plus `rdReserve(b)`.

```
law   = patentLaw(b.countryId, g.year)                              // reused from workshop/market.ts: fee, enforcement
plan  = Heavy family ? record.cost (readHeavy) : rdCost(b, family)  // a fixed price
fee   = roundBudget(0.12 x plan) + law.fee
term  = max(2, round(SHIELD[tier] x law.enforcement))               // SHIELD = low 3, mid 6, high 5
start = oldest own line of the family launched within 3 years       // g.year when it has none
until = max(g.year + 1, start + term)    years = until - g.year     // counted from the launch: a late filing buys less, never more
book  = round(IP_SHARE x plan)                                      // IP_SHARE .6: never above a fixed price
```

A Heavy family is already out of rivals' reach for its development years after the launch (`heavyLead`), so a patent adds only the years
its term outlasts that: filed at the launch, `until - heavyLead(rec)`, which is nothing for most trades and countries (a sweep of the 39
product trades in 12 countries found a patent on a real breakthrough worth more than its fee at the launch in 28 of 468: 0 of 228 low,
26 of 156 mid, 2 of 84 high). The shield tiers are starting numbers, and `patentValue` says so honestly. Counting the term from the
filing date instead would let a company that waits stretch the shield past the lead for the same fixed fee.

**Exclusivity through the field.** `shieldsOf(b)` returns `{ family, until }` for each patent that is `hold` (its `until`), or `license` with `lic` in
the future (its `lic`), and a frozen empty list when `b.lab` is absent. `fieldOf` gets a sixth parameter `market?: FieldMarket` (new:
`{ industry: string; shield?: readonly Shield[] }`, with `Shield` new: `{ family: string; until: number }`); `Field` carries it as it carries `heavy`, and the cache key gains
`|m<industry>|s<family:until,...>`. In `makeField`, a family held in that year keeps its `exists` and `level`, but no rival offers it
and the fringe doesn't either: `offer` 0, `quality` the par 50, every version -1. That is the existing novelty bonus (`edgeOf`, up to
+80%) for as long as the shield lasts, and the `heavyArrival` schedule or the adoption thresholds take over after it.

`fieldMarketOf(b)` (new, `lab-state.ts`) builds `{ industry: b.industry, shield: shieldsOf(b) }`; `marketCtxFor` and the two callers
of `rivalLines` use it, so the shield also shows on the Market screen and in a buy-out's list of lines. The shield is the holder's
own: it shapes the holder's field. Two readings that would contradict it hold back with it: `heavyArrivals` (`heavy-rd.ts`) doesn't
log a rival launching its own copy of a Heavy family while the shield of the pioneer company (looked up by `rec.by`, so an NPC
company's year-end holds it back too) lasts, and the Products row's "rivals can have their own from about N" (`leadOf`,
`manage.ts`) names the shield's last year when that is later.

**Licensing.** One irreversible action on a `hold` patent: `lic = g.year + 1` and the shield ends then. `bookLabYear` pays each year
`lic <= fiscal < until`:

```
royalty = min(capLeft, round(0.30 x plan x offer x level))    // offer, level: the family in marketCtxFor(g, b, fiscal).field
capLeft = 1.5 x plan - earned                                  // lifetime cap per patent
```

Royalties come from rivals that really sell the kind (`offer` is their pull share), so none is paid while nobody does. Rivals hold
no cash here: as with franchise royalties and studio engine licenses the money enters the statement once, is taxed, and is capped.
It is excluded from valuation earnings (4.3). **Book.** `advanceLab` multiplies each `book` by 0.9 a year (deep tech's rate), drops
a patent at `until` with a life-log line, and keeps the family in `filed`.

**One pure value function** (`patentValue(g, b, family)`, new) serves the row hint, the hired CEO and the board vote:

```
hold    = sum_{k < years} DISCOUNT^k x pullValue(ctx, b, w, pull(open field, year+k), pull(shielded field, year+k))
license = sum_{k = 1..years-1} DISCOUNT^k x min(capLeft, 0.30 x plan x offer x level)     // in the open field
value   = max(hold, license) - fee            // the larger names the best mode
```

`pull` is `productPull(...).total`; `pullValue`, `DISCOUNT` and `HORIZON` are existing `rd.ts` exports. Both fields come from `fieldOf`
with and without the shield: the resolver's own math.

### 4.7 Hints and the hired CEO (task 6)

`lab-rules.ts` holds `opsValue` (built in task 3, because the board vote needs its gain), `teamValue` (task 4), `patentValue` (task 5) and
`bestLabMove(g, b, avail, w, judge, costOnly?)` (task 6), all new, pure, no `g.rng` of their own:

```
opsValue = odds x sum_{t < HORIZON} DISCOUNT^(years - 1 + t) x yearly(t) - price
yearly   = cost:    -dCost(t) x w.cogs
           yield:   dCap(t) x w.contrib x clamp((w.util - 0.85) x 6, 0, 1)      // staffBenefit's capacity form
           quality: dDem(t) x w.contrib
d*(t)    = opsEffectsOf with the level raised by gain x .95^t, minus opsEffectsOf with the level as it is
```

`dCost`, `dCap` and `dDem` call the same `opsEffectsOf` that `businessEffects` calls, so the channel math is stated once. `w` is
`bizWorth(g, b)`. By arithmetic (not run), a restaurant's rung 1 cost program breaks even at about 3 sites.

- **Hints.** Each Operations, patent and team row has `hint` through `talentHint(g, 'Business', ...)`, gated like `hinting` in
  `manage.ts` (talent and `!manageBlock`) and cached per company state like `cachedHint`. The text reads from the functions the
  action uses: "Worth about $X on average over 8 years" or "Doesn't pay back now"; a patent row says "Keep it exclusive" or
  "License it"; a team row says what the next scientist adds a year against the pay. Hints show expected values, never a roll.
- **Hired CEO.** `productTurn` (`board-ceo.ts`) gets one block after its R&D loop: up to `LAB_PER_YEAR[mandate]` (new: growth 2,
  balanced 1, profit 1, cash 0, a cost-cutter style +1 for `cost` programs) moves from `bestLabMove(g, b, spendable(t), bizWorth(g, b),
  t.judge)`. Each is paid with `spend(t, price, true)`, so the board's `capexLeft` and the reserve limit it, then done by `startOps` or
  `fileLabPatent` (new, the patent counterpart) with `pay` false, the spend being already taken. The CEO files in the mode `patentValue` prefers, and never licenses later or hires. The CEO's `judge` draws one number from
  `g.rng` per call, so `bestLabMove` calls it only for a candidate whose exact value is above 0: a hired CEO with nothing worth
  starting draws exactly what it drew in v3.0.0, and the seeded outcomes of such saves don't shift.
- **Menu actions.** Each goes through `manageBlock`, re-reads the live company, re-checks its `*Block`, and for any spend calls
  `boardAllows(g, cur, bb, { kind: 'capex', by: 'owner', amount, gain, label })` with `gain = max(0, win / 5)`, ordinary R&D's
  convention (`win` is the gross expected win before its price: `rdValue.value + budget` there, here `opsValue + price`,
  `patentValue.value + fee`, `teamValue.gain`). A release asks with `gain` 0 (it spends the fee); a license spends nothing and asks no vote. The library entry points (`doOps`, `doPatent`, `doLicense`, `doHire`, `doRelease`, all new in `lab-year.ts`) hold
  these checks, so a direct call, a restored menu or an older save can't skip them.

### 4.8 Rival R&D (task 7)

A rival's version of a kind rises every year it has sold it. Pure, by hash, no `g.rng`:

```
rise = min(RISE_CAP, RISE_BASE[tier] x RISE_SIZE[rival.tier] x (0.6 + 0.8 x mix(hashOf(r.id), hashOf(family), 5)) x yearsSelling)
RISE_BASE = low .20, mid .30, high .45, breakthrough .60 (points a year)    RISE_SIZE = giant 1.3, major 1.15, mid 1, small .7
RISE_CAP = 8 points      yearsSelling <= RISE_SPAN = 12
```

`riseOf` (new, `product-market.ts`) is added to a classic version's quality in `makeField` and in `rivalLines`, as
`Math.round(Math.min(99, quality + rise))` (versions are whole numbers today and the Market screen prints them as they are), so the
Market screen and the field agree. `rivalLines` also takes an optional fifth argument, the shields of 4.6, and lists no held family. `yearsSelling` counts back from `year` while `demandLevel(...).adoption` is at least the rival's
threshold; `makeField` reads a kind's 12-year adoption series once for all rivals. For a Heavy kind it is `year - heavyArrival(rec, r)`
and the version never exceeds `rec.quality` (the "never above the pioneer" rule stays). The field key gains the industry (4.6),
because the tier now changes the numbers. A field built without `market` has no rise and no shield (the v3.0.0 numbers); `fieldFor`'s
bare-context fallback passes `{ industry: ctx.industry }`, and every other call site passes `field.market`. `rivalLines` always
carries the rise (it has the industry), which keeps the Market screen and a buy-out quote on one number.

The player's lines still age by `AGE_RATE`; the rise is on top and bounded at 8 points. A mid rival in a high trade adds .45 points a
year to a gap that already closes at 2, so ten years cost about 4.5 points, an edge of about 0.86. Task 7 pins this with a test over
all 55 trades.

### 4.9 Menus and where they plug in

`labMenus(a, b)` (new, `lab-menu.ts`) returns `[]` unless `heavyEligible(b)` and is added after `productsMenu(a)` in `businessView`
(`manage.ts`), one line. The screen, id `biz.lab.<assetId>`:

- Header: the tier, the country's credit rule ("R&D credit: 30% of what R&D started this year cost") and last year's lab ledger.
- Operations R&D: a row per running program (years left, odds), then one action per program on offer, subtitle "$57K · 2 years · 74%
  odds · unit costs fall up to 4.0%" (the last clause by track: "unit costs fall up to N%", "output rises up to N%", "demand
  rises up to N%", N from the same `opsEffectsOf` cap the year-end uses), and a confirm that repeats price, years and odds.
  Without a credit in the country the header says "No R&D credit here." in place of the credit rule.
- Patents: one action per candidate family, meaning the company's own lines launched in the last 3 years, newest first, at most 6
  (`patentableFamilies`; "$7.2K · rivals can't sell one for 6 years", or the single `patentBlock` reason when it can't be filed),
  one row per patent with years left, and "License <family>" on a `hold` patent.
- Research team: one info row (n of cap, pay, odds and slots), "Hire a Scientist", "Let a Scientist Go".

A row that can't run carries the single reason from its `*Block` function, as plain text, so keyboard and screen-reader users get
what a tap gets.

### 4.10 Events, news and copy

Life-log lines (cap 90), outcomes and notices (120), tooltips (120), per `docs/VOICE.md`: contractions, no encouragement, no
em-dash character anywhere, a number beats an adjective. No event scene (cap 140) is added. `${}` counts as zero:

- `${name} started ${program}.`, `${name}'s ${program} worked.`, `${name}'s ${program} didn't work.`, `${name} hired a research scientist.`
- `${name} patented ${family}.`, `${name}'s patent on ${family} ran out.`, `${name} licensed ${family} to rivals.`
- `${name} let a research scientist go.`, `${name} received a ${amount} research grant.`, `${name} earned its first ${amount} in royalties on ${family}.`
- Notice "Operations R&D": `${name} is working on ${program}. It costs ${price}, takes ${n} years and has ${p}% odds. If it fails, the money is gone.`
- Notice "Patent Filed": `${name} filed for a patent on ${family}. For ${n} years rivals can't sell one. The fee was ${fee}.`
- Notice "Licensed": `Rivals can sell ${family} from ${year}. You earn royalties while they do, until ${until}.` (royalties need rivals
  that really sell it and stop at the cap, so the notice promises no payment)
- Statement labels: "R&D tax credit and grants", "Patent licences", "Research team".

Notices go to the owner only when `runsIt(g, a)` and the character is alive, as `advanceRd` does. A grant and a first royalty each
write one life-log line. The credit is a statement line, not a log line, so it doesn't repeat every year. Rival R&D has no news item:
it shows in the existing "vs rivals' 61" text, which reads from the field. A test measures every template against the caps.

## 6. Determinism and the roadmap invariants

| Invariant | How it holds |
|---|---|
| No foresight | Hints show expected values from stored odds. Rival rise is a hash schedule the planner already reads for every future year, not a draw. |
| Deterministic | The only `g.rng` draw is `advanceLab`'s `chance(g, odds)`, one per finished program in track order, and none without a program. Menus, quotes, hints, `riseOf`, `shieldsOf` and `patentValue` use hashes and stored state only. |
| Accounting conservation | Credit, grant, royalty: `b.cash` plus `b.yearIncome`. Payroll, fees, prices: `b.cash` plus `b.yearCosts`. Each has a ledger amount and a statement line. Royalty's source is the rivals' pull (no cash entity), capped per patent. |
| Action-level authorization | `doOps`, `doPatent`, `doLicense`, `doHire`, `doRelease` check `manageBlock`, the `*Block` reason and `boardAllows` themselves. |
| Save compatibility, bounded state | Optional `lab`, normalized. 3 programs, 6 patents, 40 filed names, 6 scientists, 4 ledger rows, 9 `done` ids. No world flag. |
| Yearly idempotence | `bookLabYear` is guarded by `booked`, `advanceLab` by `stepped`; levels and the shield fade by year arithmetic, not by a step. |
| Pure presentation, honest hints | Menus read `readBiz` and pure functions; a test deep-freezes state and compares `g.rng` before and after. Hint, CEO and board gain call `opsValue`, `patentValue`, `bestLabMove`. |
| Truthful copy | Every number in a subtitle or hint is the stored odds, price or an `opsEffectsOf` cap; ceilings say "up to". |

## 7. Balance guards and exploit risks

| Risk | Guard | Test |
|---|---|---|
| Price tied to cash or size | Price is `rdSiteScale` x rung, fixed per trade | companies with 1x and 1000x cash and sales quote the same price |
| Benefit scales with the company, the price doesn't | Ceilings 4-10% per channel, fade 5% a year, 20% less per win | level never above .95; effects within the caps |
| Stacking with supplier, strategies, executive edge | `businessEffects` clamps stay the outer bound; the ops cost cap is 10% | worst-case stack stays at or above .65 |
| Credit farming | Under 50% of spend, capped at 250,000, no scientist pay, deep tech excluded | credit never exceeds spend, all 30 countries |
| Grant stacking | 3-year gap, at most 25 sites, `min(share, .5 - rate)`, paid after the work | credit plus grant at most 50% of price in every country |
| Patent as a market lock | Novelty test, 2 to 7 years counted from the launch (a late filing never stretches it), no refiling, at most 6, low tier only on breakthroughs | shield ends on time and on the same year whenever it is filed; a second filing is refused |
| Licensing as a pump | One-way switch, 1.5x plan lifetime cap, royalty only where rivals sell, excluded from valuation earnings | royalty never exceeds the cap; valuation moves only by `book` |
| IP valuation pump | Book is .6 x a fixed price, x.9 a year, zero at expiry | file, expire: valuation returns to where it was |
| Hire, start, release | 3-year minimum service, fee both ways, odds fixed at project start | release before 3 years refused |
| Rival rise breaks calibration | Cap 8 points, never above the pioneer, tier-scaled | rise within 0 and 8 over 12 years for all trades |
| CEO spam | `LAB_PER_YEAR`, `spendable(t)`, board `capexLeft`, `t.judge` | a cash mandate makes none |

- **Performance.** The rise reads 12 adoption values per kind per field build (memoized); `shieldsOf` is a frozen empty list without
  `lab`; hints are cached per company state. A test bounds field builds in a planner run.
- **Starting numbers.** Ceilings and prices are pinned by tests as invariants, not exact values, so integration can tune them.

  versions of every trade (deep tech's `breakthrough` tier too, through `marketCtxFor`). No existing assertion pins an aged line of a

  computation in `heavy-engine.test.ts` is edited in task 7). Suites that play those trades for years (`economy-sectors`, the
