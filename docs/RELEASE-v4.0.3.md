# AnotherLife v4.0.3

Fourteen fixes to money, laws, heirs and text. Saves from v4.0.2 load unchanged.

## Fixes

- **Bought followers paid like real ones.** A $70,000 pack of 1,000,000 followers could bring in
  over $3 million a year. Sponsor offers, the follower gates and game sales now count only real
  followers. Each year 30% of the bought ones drop away, and creator pay has a ceiling. The account
  header shows how many are bought.
- **The free analyst tip read next year.** It called next year's move right about two times in
  three. It now reads this year's returns and is a coin flip. The Investing talent's gut feeling
  is the only look ahead.
- **A 100-rated CEO guaranteed a profit.** A hired CEO or group CEO at skill 100 covered a missed
  year with money from nowhere. That is gone. A loss year is a loss year, and the delegation help
  says no rating guarantees a profit.
- **Heirs lost a President's laws.** The taxes, tariffs and institutions you set as President
  vanished when a child took over. They now carry over. The office doesn't: the heir starts with
  no term.
- **A sibling who inherited the family firm lost it.** The sibling your parent named got the firm's
  value as cash, and the firm was gone within years. The sibling now keeps the firm. A mortgaged
  or co-owned firm, or a sibling under 21 or already running a company, still turns into cash.
- **A tariff cut drew retaliation.** Cutting a tariff from 20% to 10% was treated like a new tariff
  and could draw a hit back. A cut now ends the retaliation, and the log says it is rolling back.
- **A divorce took more than the judge ordered.** The ex got the whole value of each sold asset. A
  player with $100,000 and a $5,000,000 house lost it all on an order of $1.275M to $2.55M. The ex
  now gets the ordered amount and you keep the rest. A shortfall at the end is still forgiven.
- **Deals and Olympic bonuses ignored the year and the country.** A brand now pays at most 40
  typical local incomes for each point of its fame, in the year's money. A 1932 Singapore gold
  paid $740,000 and now pays $229,000. US pay from 2026 is unchanged, and signed deals keep
  their pay.
- **Author adaptation fees and royalties had no ceiling.** A fee could reach $7.5M, and a royalty
  $1.8M a year for five years. The fee is now capped at 150 typical local incomes and the yearly
  base at 15 (US 2026: $6.75M and $675,000. US 1930: $2,025,000 and $202,500).
- **Studio awards could pin a game.** A game with several awards could never be trimmed from the
  back catalogue. Awards now count for at most two. Nothing awards a game yet, so no current save
  changes.
- **The "avoiding me" line repeated.** A debtor who never repaid could say it 6 to 10 times in 20
  years. The same person now says it at most once in five years. The relationship drop is the
  same.
- **Some text was hard to read.** The muted grey, the green and red tones and the age heading in
  the light theme missed the 4.5 to 1 contrast that WCAG AA asks for on some cards. They now pass
  on every background. The dark theme was already fine.
- **Military pay and Street Hustler takes ignored the era and the country.** A private earned
  $21,000 in 1930. Pay now scales with the typical income of the year and country: 0.3 times in
  the US in 1930, 0.2 times in Nigeria today. US pay from 2026 is unchanged, and a job you hold
  keeps its salary.
- **A podcast audience had no ceiling.** At high fame it reached billions of listeners and
  hundreds of billions a year in ads. It now stops at 30 million, about the size of the biggest
  real shows. Ad income follows the era and country like military pay. Book and merch pay don't.
- **A bank CEO repeated its capital choices.** If a hired bank CEO ran twice in one year, the second
  run bought another product line, opened another branch and started another campaign. One test
  bank spent a further $678,000. These choices are now made once a year. Investment firms and fund
  managers already did this, and tests now hold them to it.
- **A bank CEO could plan on the wrong year.** A bank whose books skipped years planned on a year
  the economy no longer keeps, and a damaged year stamp could point at the future. The CEO now
  plans on last year or this year, never an older or later one.
- **Rival and venture lists could grow without limit in a damaged save.** On load, rival modifiers
  and rival shares are kept to 40 each and live startups in a venture fund to 200. Normal play
  peaks near 70 startups, so only a damaged or edited save changes.

## For players with old saves

Nothing in a v4.0.2 save needs converting. A few saved numbers read differently:

- Followers you bought in v4.0.2 weren't tracked, so they load as real followers. Bought followers
  start at 0 and are counted from your next purchase.
- A saved adaptation base above the new ceiling pays at most the ceiling.
- A saved podcast audience above 30 million loads at 30 million.
- Jobs, deals and statements you already hold keep what they saved. An old Profit and Loss row for
  trading income still shows.
- Rival modifiers, rival shares and live startups beyond the new limits are cut on load. A save
  from normal play has none.
