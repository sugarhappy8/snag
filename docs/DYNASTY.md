# Main Character Syndrome & Family Conglomerates

Main Character Syndrome passes to every child of a gifted character, including
adopted children and stepchildren. Linked grandchildren inherit it from gifted
parents too. Existing saves are upgraded without charging money or rerolling RNG.
Taking over a child's life preserves the talent; there is no inheritance lottery.

## Independent children

The child's Relationships profile shows the inherited talent, professional skill,
career history, and (when their life is known to you) an ambition score out of 100.
The gift produces first-use mastery of schoolwork and professional skills, with
childhood age ceilings. Adult mastery also feeds into family employment, management,
company planning, and investment performance. It does not fabricate degrees,
remove legal age gates, or grant startup money.

- Below 65 ambition: talent helps their chosen career; no gifted-founder push.
- 65–79: may start an independent company using personal savings.
- 80–100: may deliberately enter an affordable industry where your business operates.
  More ambition increases the yearly chance of taking this step.

NPC businesses pay startup costs, payroll, expenses, and taxes; cash and personal
savings remain separate. Actual firms enter the demand model as competitors on
both sides, within national/world market boundaries. A rival's market strength
comes from its funded capacity and recorded sales. Negotiate with an NPC founder
through Relationships, not the generic fictional-rival acquisition menu.

Independent startups currently use the operating-business model (including financial
firms), not the separate game-launch or deep-tech research workflows. Those specialized
classes are excluded from autonomous founding rather than created as empty shells.

## Succession

When you continue as a child after death, a conglomerate stays one holding. Your
ownership divides according to the will, or equally among living children when
there is no named will. Explicit single-child succession bequests still apply.
All divisions, company cash, treasury designation, and existing hired management
remain together. Minors inherit shares too; management continues on their behalf.

Family co-owners have durable ownership records, not a duplicate cash payment for
the value of the company. Their shares contribute to their visible wealth, receive
dividends, are protected from automatic buybacks, and dilute proportionally when
new shares are issued. Older co-owners keep their stakes when the next player dies:
only that player's own stake is divided again. An excluded playable child can
still see a preserved private family holding, but owns none of its value. A listed
company or group the player owns none of (an excluded heir's, or one the player sold
out of) leaves the player's companies: each relative's shares become part of their
own wealth at the market price, paid through the same routing as dividends.

Conglomerate shares are transferred in kind outside the cash-estate liquidation
pool, matching the game's existing specific-business-bequest treatment. Cash,
ordinary property, will disputes, and probate retain their existing settlement rules.
Co-owners' interests also survive an intentional division spin-off, and division
sale/closure proceeds pay known family owners their share.
